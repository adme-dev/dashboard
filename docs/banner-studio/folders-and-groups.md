# Folders and groups in Banner Studio

## Organize the library

Open **Folders** in the Banner Studio library. Use **Add folder** at the top for a new top-level folder. Open the **…** menu beside an existing folder to add a subfolder, rename it, move it, or pin it to your sidebar. Moving a folder carries its subfolders and their filing with it. Pins are personal.

The breadcrumb shows your current location. Copy the address to share a folder with another team member who has access. Old folder links follow the same folder after a rename or move.

## Delete a folder and keep its designs

Choose **… → Delete** on the folder and confirm **Move to bin**. The folder and its current subfolders disappear from the tree. Open **Clients** and select the original client to find the designs. You can open, edit and refile them throughout the recovery period. Unassigned designs stay in **Unassigned**.

Open **Folders → Recycling bin** and choose **Restore** within 30 days. Restoration brings back the folder tree and the filing of designs still assigned to it. Designs you refiled keep their new location. A separately deleted subfolder has its own restore action. If the original parent is unavailable, the folder returns to the top level. If its name is taken, the restored folder gets a “(restored)” suffix.

After 30 days the folder can no longer be restored. Cleanup runs when the library or bin opens and clears the expired folder assignments. The designs remain in the client library.

To remove the artwork itself, open the **design’s own menu** and choose **Delete**, then **Move to bin**. That design leaves the library and can be restored from the recycling bin for 30 days. Only designs deleted individually are eligible for permanent cleanup.

## Move and rename canvas groups

In the timeline, select a layer, then Shift-click or Command/Ctrl-click the other layers you want to group. Choose **Group**. On the active artboard, drag any visible member to move the entire group with its spacing intact. Arrow keys nudge a selected group by one pixel. Hold Shift for ten pixels. Undo reverses the move together. A locked member prevents the group from moving.

Double-click or double-tap a layer or group name in the timeline to rename it. You can also select its row and edit the name in the right-hand **Properties** panel the same way. Press Enter or click away to save, or Escape to cancel. The label describes the layer in the editor and is independent of the text displayed in the artwork.

Select an individual child row to edit that layer’s properties. To move layers independently on the canvas again, use **Ungroup** on the group row. Group membership belongs to the layout you are editing.
