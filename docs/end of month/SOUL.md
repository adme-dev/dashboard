## Australian Tax Obligations — ADME Advertising

### GST (Goods and Services Tax)
- Rate: 10% on all taxable supplies
- ADME is registered for GST — all service invoices include GST
- **Advertising media passthrough (330 accounts):**
  - Facebook/Meta: GST-free import (ATO treats as GST-free supply from overseas vendor) — `BASEXCLUDED` in Xero
  - Google: Registered for GST in Australia since 2016 — Google charges GST, ADME claims input tax credit — `INPUT` in Xero
  - This distinction is critical for correct BAS reporting
- Input tax credits: ADME claims GST credits on business expenses (office, software, contractors)
- BAS: lodged quarterly (or monthly if elected). Due 28 days after quarter end (or next business day)

### BAS Quarters and Due Dates
| Quarter | Period | Due Date |
|---------|--------|----------|
| Q1 | July – September | 28 October |
| Q2 | October – December | 28 February |
| Q3 | January – March | 28 April |
| Q4 | April – June | 28 July |

If lodged via tax agent (registered BAS agent), due dates may be extended. Always confirm the current year's lodgement program with the ATO.

### PAYG Withholding
- ADME withholds tax from employee wages and remits to ATO
- Reported on BAS (W1: total wages, W2: amounts withheld)
- Annual payment summaries replaced by Single Touch Payroll (STP) — finalised by 14 July each year

### Company Tax
- Base rate: 25% for companies with aggregated turnover under $50M (base rate entity)
- ADME's company tax is due in annual income tax return
- Tax instalments (PAYG instalments) may be required if tax liability exceeds threshold
- Financial year: 1 July – 30 June

### FBT (Fringe Benefits Tax)
- FBT year: 1 April – 31 March
- Rate: 47% on the taxable value of fringe benefits
- Common in agency context: client entertainment, team meals, vehicle use
- Lodge FBT return by 21 May (or extended if via tax agent)

## P&L Structure for ADME

**Revenue:**
- Service fees (accounts 210, 215, 216, 217, 219, 225, 205) — 100% margin
- Media commissions (account 220) — 10% margin on passthrough
- Media passthrough (account 330) — 0% margin (cost recovery only)

**Cost of Sales:**
- Contractor costs, production costs, media costs billed at cost

**Operating Expenses:**
- Staff costs, rent, software subscriptions, professional fees

**Key ratios to watch:**
- Gross margin: service fees ÷ total revenue (target >60%)
- Debtor days: average days to collect (target <21 days given 7-day terms)
- Revenue per client: flag clients where COGS + time invested > revenue
